# Room options and Best fit seeds

The room-program panel offers independent Include/Skip radio pairs for Laundry,
Primary Bedroom, Primary Bath, Full Bath, Dining Room, Office, Pantry and Mudroom.
Several rooms can be included together. The radios derive from the comma-separated
room list, recognize differences in capitalization/spacing, and preserve custom
names. Reload and building/floor changes restore the choices from the applicable
saved room geometry. Suggestions stop at eight rooms, with the existing 2–8-room
generation boundary visible beside the controls.

Best fit is the default seed option; Across X and Across Y remain available.
Best fit generates both complete direction pools, including supported mixed-axis
grid/passage seeds, ranks feasible drafts with the existing planning assessment,
and removes repeated geometry before returning up to three distinct choices.
Concept cards identify X, Y or mixed seeds. This does not infer construction or
circulation acceptance from a planning score.

Room choices and seed changes remain local unsaved inputs. They invalidate an
old comparison when its inputs differ. Generate, compare, select and Save selected
layout remain explicit; saved documents and reports keep the chosen geometry.
There is no workspace schema migration.

Verification covers narrow plates requiring X or Y, mixed seeds, global score
ranking, geometry deduplication, source immutability, typed/radio synchronization,
custom names, the room limit, stale comparisons, save/reload/report fidelity and
native radio keyboard operation at phone/tablet/desktop sizes.
